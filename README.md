# SnapChart-AI
Personal AI Chart Analysis and Chat App

Upload a chart screenshot and the app marks BOS, CHoCH, MSS, i-BOS, i-MSS, FVG, IFVG, POI, AOI, Supply/Demand, BSL/SSL, Sweep and Weak high/low automatically — pro SMC style, even on real phone screenshots with small candles and hand-drawn lines — offline, on your device. Fictional practice worksheets with BOS/MSS/FVG answers are built in.
